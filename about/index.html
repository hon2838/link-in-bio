About Mathew

My full name is Hon Jun Yoon, and most people know me as Mathew Hon. Based in Malaysia, I work across physical infrastructure, computer hardware, and software systems.

How I Approach Technology

My interest in computing started with the physical hardware itself. Since 2016, I have independently built, upgraded, and troubleshot desktop and laptop PCs—learning firsthand how power delivery, thermals, firmware, memory stability, and storage interact long before an operating system even boots.

Over time, that curiosity expanded upward through the stack: from component diagnostics and community IT support into Linux environments, enterprise server administration, network routing, cloud architecture, and full-stack web applications.

Today, my day-to-day work places me inside mission-critical data center facilities in Cyberjaya, where reliability is measured in continuous power, cooling telemetry, and disciplined operational response.

Outside of work, I enjoy tinkering with Linux workstations, automating repetitive workflows, exploring cloud infrastructure, and experimenting with local language models and self-hosted software.
